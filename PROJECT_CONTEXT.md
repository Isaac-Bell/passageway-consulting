## Purpose of the website

The site should help a visitor quickly understand:

1. what Passageway believes;
2. how emotional health and life consulting can support her;
3. who Hannah and Kimberly are;
4. which one-to-one, workshop, or cohort pathway fits;
5. what sessions cost; and
6. how to book or take a gentle first step.

The primary conversion is an appointment on the external Acuity booking page.
Secondary conversions are joining the cohort interest list, downloading the
Self-Compassion Reflection Guide, and joining the future newsletter.

## Audience

Passageway primarily serves women seeking emotional-health support, greater
self-acceptance, nervous-system regulation, confidence, boundaries, a stronger
voice, and personal freedom. Visitors may be navigating anxiety,
people-pleasing, low self-esteem, religious trauma, unhealthy relationship
patterns, dating or singleness, and seasons of change.

Use welcoming, inclusive language that lets a visitor arrive without already
having the right words. Do not assume that she sees herself as broken or needs
to be fixed.

The exact geographic service area and the balance of online versus in-person
services still need to be confirmed before location-specific claims are added.

## Brand voice

The voice should be:

- compassionate, warm, safe, and human;
- grounded rather than clinical or corporate;
- truthful and hopeful without being sugary;
- empowering without blaming people for their pain;
- gently faith-aware where it belongs, without making every sentence religious;
- clear enough for someone who is tired, anxious, or uncertain; and
- emotionally resonant without making exaggerated promises.

Prefer phrases such as “you make sense,” “you are not broken,” “you are allowed
to choose something new,” and “we will meet you where you are.” Avoid fear-based
sales language, urgency tricks, miracle claims, jargon, and keyword stuffing.

## Scope and safety

Passageway offers consulting, education, workshops, cohorts, and personal
support. The site must not imply that Passageway provides psychotherapy,
diagnosis, crisis care, or medical treatment unless the business later confirms
appropriately licensed services and wording.

Keep the existing FAQ distinction that Passageway is not therapy. Health,
trauma, anxiety, nervous-system, and healing language should remain supportive
and educational, not diagnostic or guaranteed. Avoid claims such as “cure,”
“treat,” or guaranteed outcomes.

Preserve these principles:

## Technical architecture

- Next.js 16 and React 19
- TypeScript
- Vinext/Vite output targeting a Cloudflare Worker runtime
- ChatGPT Sites project identity stored in `.openai/hosting.json`
- Production build: `npm run build`
- Full validation: `npm test`
- Node.js requirement: `>=22.13.0`
- GitHub Actions workflow: `.github/workflows/quality.yml`

The build produces the validated Worker/server artifact and static assets used
by Sites. Do not replace the hosting manifest, Vinext architecture, lockfile, or
verified build scripts without a specific technical reason.

GitHub Actions automatically runs the production build and tests for every pull
request into `main` and every push to `main`. A successful push to `main` also
retains the `dist/` deployment artifact for 14 days. This is an automatic build
and quality gate; it does not automatically publish a new ChatGPT Sites version.

## Source and release workflow

1. Begin from the latest GitHub `main`.
2. Create an `agent/<short-description>` branch.
3. Make only the approved changes and preserve unrelated work.
4. Run the relevant build, tests, responsive/accessibility checks, and visual
   review.
5. Open a draft pull request describing the content and user impact. GitHub
   Actions must complete the `Build and test` check.
6. Review and merge the PR into GitHub `main`; the workflow builds `main` again
   and retains its artifact.
7. Deliberately deploy the merged `main` through the existing ChatGPT Sites
   project.
8. Verify the deployment and `https://passagewayconsulting.com`.

GitHub is the canonical code and content history. ChatGPT Sites holds the
deployed versions, domain connection, and built-in traffic totals. Temporary
agent workspaces may be cleaned between sessions and must not be treated as
durable storage.

## SEO launch plan

The custom domain is new and was not found by a `site:passagewayconsulting.com`
search on August 6, 2026. Indexing and meaningful ranking should be treated as
an intentional launch phase, not an overnight refresh.

### Technical SEO priorities

- Create and verify a Google Search Console **domain property** using a Porkbun
  DNS TXT record.
- Add and submit `sitemap.xml`.
- Add or verify `robots.txt` and ensure production pages are indexable.
- Set the canonical site URL and consistent canonical tags to the custom domain.
- Improve unique titles, meta descriptions, social sharing metadata, image alt
  text, and semantic heading structure.
- Add truthful JSON-LD structured data for the organization and, once location
  details are confirmed, the appropriate local business/service information.
- Validate structured data and inspect the live URL through Search Console.
- Track Core Web Vitals and preserve fast mobile performance.

### Content and local-search priorities

- Target specific high-intent phrases before broad national terms, for example
  “emotional health consulting Kansas City,” “women's emotional health
  workshops Kansas City,” “nervous system regulation support for women,” and
  “self-compassion coaching for women.” Final wording depends on confirmed
  location and service format.
- Develop useful, substantial pages for one-to-one consulting, workshops,
  cohorts, the founders, guiding truths, and resources. Give each page a clear
  search purpose and unique metadata.
- Publish people-first articles and resources that reflect Hannah and
  Kimberly's real experience rather than generic keyword content.
- Create or complete the appropriate Google Business Profile if the business
  is eligible, keeping name, service area, website, and contact details
  consistent.
- Ask trusted partners already mentioning Passageway, including relevant local
  wellness organizations, to link to the custom domain.
- Link the domain from approved social profiles, newsletters, workshop listings,
  and local directories.

No one can guarantee a number-one Google ranking. Assess indexing in days to
weeks and organic ranking progress over weeks to months, then iterate using
Search Console evidence rather than guesses.

## Analytics and reporting plan

ChatGPT Sites automatically provides total unique visitors, total page views,
and both metrics over time. Use it for a quick traffic pulse.

For deeper measurement, the recommended next step is a Passageway-owned Google
Analytics 4 property plus Google Search Console. GA4 should be configured with
privacy-conscious measurement and useful named events rather than collecting
data without a decision attached.

Recommended reporting metrics:

- users, sessions, and page views;
- landing pages and most-viewed pages;
- average engagement time and engagement rate;
- traffic source/medium, campaign, and referring site;
- approximate country/city and device category;
- Google search queries, impressions, clicks, click-through rate, and average
  position from Search Console;
- booking-button clicks, including which section or consultant generated them;
- PDF guide downloads;
- newsletter-signup attempts and completions once Mailchimp is connected;
- cohort-interest clicks or submissions; and
- changes versus the previous week or month.

Do not send sensitive form content, health concerns, email addresses, names, or
booking details to analytics. Use aggregated event names and counts.

GA4 reports can be scheduled to approved recipients weekly or monthly as PDF or
CSV attachments. A concise monthly report is the recommended starting cadence;
weekly reporting will become more useful once traffic and active campaigns grow.
